Welcome to INEEDYOU (Pty) Ltd, trading as I NEED YOU Authentic Wear - a message-driven streetwear brand built by South African youth entrepreneurs and made in South Africa.

Where we started

INEEDYOU Authentic Wear was founded in 2025 in Mohlakeng, Gauteng. The label started lean, using pre-orders and print-on-demand instead of buying bulk stock. Our first drop was a white hoodie, oversized tees, sleeveless tops and tracksuits, all made in South Africa.

Looking for the full story, mission and vision? Visit the About page.

What are we selling?

Hoodies, tees, vests and tracksuits, plus custom printing for events. Search and sort our range on the Products page.

Get in touch

Reach us on Instagram or send a message through our Enquiry form. Full contact details and a map are on the Contact page.
